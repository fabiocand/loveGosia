>>From this moment, we will no longer be separated.

You live inside my heart

this imperfect place,
and you chose to stay
even though the walls are cracked
and the floor still trembles.

I didn’t promise you the moon,
only the warmth of my chest
and all the disorder I carry within it.
And still, you stay.

You light gentle lamps
where darkness had taken root,
and with patient hands
you gather the rubble of my past
as if it were something precious,
just to make room.

You live inside my heart,
this fragile shelter,
and for the first time
I no longer feel the urge
to tear it down.

I only want to learn
how to make it a little more beautiful
for you.

Stay.
Love these cracks,
because that’s where the light gets in.

I already love you,
precisely because
you chose to live in me.

I watched her come down the stairs and something in me went quiet — I couldn’t get a word out for a full minute.

My brain just stopped.

Only one thought made it through:

“I love her.”
